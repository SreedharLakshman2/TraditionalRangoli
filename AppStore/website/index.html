Traditional Rangoli Support

iPhone and iPad · iOS 17 or later · Lifestyle

This page is support for Traditional Rangoli, a courtyard companion for Indian rangoli and Tamil kolam. Discover patterns, learn step by step, draw with symmetry, decorate, then save on this iPhone or iPad. There is no account. The app is free and shows ads.

Need help?

Email sreedharlakshmanan4@gmail.com or read the Privacy Policy.

Getting started

  • Open Daily Rangoli on Home, then Learn Step-by-Step or Start Drawing.
  • On a dot grid, strokes snap toward nearby pulli. Undo, redo, and symmetry live in the toolbar.
  • After Done, color enclosed areas or add rice powder, flowers, and diyas.
  • Save to My Rangolis. Share uses the iOS share sheet.

Ads

A Google AdMob banner can appear at the bottom of the main tabs. A full-screen ad may show after you finish a rangoli so the app can stay free. iOS may ask for tracking permission so ads can be more relevant. Allow or decline; the app still works either way.
