Privacy Policy for Traditional Rangoli

Last updated: August 25, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.TraditionalRangoli

Traditional Rangoli is a rangoli and kolam learning app for iPhone and iPad. Drawings, favorites, and progress stay on your device. There is no account. The app is free and shows third-party ads.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Traditional Rangoli under the Sreeo Studio brand. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

  • Rangoli drawings, thumbnails, and favorites
  • XP, streak, completed patterns, sound, haptics, grid, and onboarding
  • Local timestamps used only to decide when to show Apple’s rating prompt

Ratings go through Apple’s StoreKit dialog. We do not receive your star rating or review text.

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies. There is no account or login.

App ratings

After you complete a few rangoli, or if you tap Rate Traditional Rangoli in Profile, the app may show Apple’s in-app rating card. We do not receive your rating. Apple limits how often that card can appear.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show a banner at the bottom of the main tabs and a full-screen ad after you complete a rangoli.

On iOS 14.5 and later we may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission so ads can be more relevant. You can decline. The app still works, and ads can still appear.

Google may collect a device identifier and related advertising data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads. That data is not linked to an account we hold.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google’s advertising page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Authorized sellers are listed at https://sreedharlakshman2.github.io/app-ads.txt (and the matching ads.txt at the same site root). AdMob verifies this app against that root file.

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No email, phone number, or account
  • No contacts, photo library, precise location, or files outside the app
  • No Sai Laksha Technologies analytics or crash reporter
  • We do not sell your data or upload artwork to our servers

Sharing

If you share a rangoli, iOS’s share sheet may send that image to the app you choose.

Children’s privacy

Traditional Rangoli is intended for a general audience including families. It is not in Apple’s Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

App Store privacy label

Device ID and advertising data may be used by Google AdMob for third-party advertising. Device ID may be used to track you across apps if you allow tracking. None of this is linked to an identity we hold.

Data retention and deletion

All drawings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app. There is no cloud backup.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
