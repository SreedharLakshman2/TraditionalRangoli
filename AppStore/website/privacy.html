Privacy Policy for Traditional Rangoli

Last updated: August 25, 2026 · Bundle ID com.sreedhar.TraditionalRangoli

Traditional Rangoli is a rangoli and kolam learning app. Drawings, favorites, and progress stay on your device. There is no account and no advertising.

Who publishes this app

Sai Laksha Technologies publishes Traditional Rangoli. Developer: Sreedhar Lakshmanan.

Information stored on your device

  • Rangoli drawings, thumbnails, and favorites
  • XP, streak, completed patterns, sound, haptics, grid, and onboarding

This is not sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

Information we do not collect ourselves

  • No email, phone number, or account
  • No contacts, photo library, precise location, or files outside the app
  • No analytics, crash reporter, or ads SDK
  • We do not sell your data or upload artwork to our servers

Sharing

If you share a rangoli, iOS’s share sheet may send that image to the app you choose.

Children’s privacy

Traditional Rangoli is intended for a general audience including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

All drawings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app.
