## Description

Traditional Rangoli is a calm, premium companion for Indian rangoli and Tamil kolam.

Discover lotus, peacock, diya, mandala, Pongal and Onam patterns. Learn each one stroke by stroke on a pulli grid, then draw freely with 4-way and 8-way symmetry that mirrors a single powder line into a full courtyard piece.

• Daily rangoli and 16 traditional patterns  
• Guided tracing that is kind, not strict  
• Dot-grid, freehand, and template studios  
• Rice powder, flower, and diya decoration  
• Save, favorite, duplicate, and share from your gallery  
• XP journey, streaks, and courtyard achievements  

Artwork stays on your device. No account. A banner sits on the main tabs, and a full-screen ad may appear after you finish a rangoli. After a few completed designs, Apple’s in-app rating card may appear; you can also rate from Profile.

Works on iPhone (portrait) and iPad (portrait and landscape).
