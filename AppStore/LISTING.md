## Description

Learn Kolam is a course, not a rangoli sketchpad.

The first screen is today's lesson: a Tamil name, why that kolam is drawn at the door, the pulli count, and Learn Step-by-Step. There is no Create tab and no freehand studio until you finish one guided lesson. Home is the course — coming lessons are listed under today, not a pattern gallery.

Each lesson places the dots, then asks you to trace. The check is kind, not a quiz. After that first lesson, you can keep the line, decorate with rice powder, flowers, and diyas, and save on this device.

• First launch = today's lesson only
• Festival-aware course (Pongal, Deepavali, Onam, or the everyday threshold kolam)
• Tamil and English names on every pattern
• Guided tracing on a pulli grid
• Pulli, sikku, Pongal pot, Onam pookalam, and more (16 original geometries)
• Studio unlocks after one completed lesson
• 21 languages
• Save and share from an on-device gallery — no account

Artwork stays on your device. A banner sits on the main tabs, and a full-screen ad may appear after you finish a lesson. After a few completed designs, Apple's in-app rating card may appear; you can also rate from Profile.

Works on iPhone (portrait) and iPad (portrait and landscape).
