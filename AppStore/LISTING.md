## Description

Learn Tamil pulli and sikku kolam stroke by stroke.

Traditional Rangoli is a courtyard teacher, not a coloring book and not a generic symmetry sketchpad. Each pattern has a Tamil name, a pulli count, and a short note on why that kolam is drawn at the door. Home opens today's lesson — harvest week, Deepavali, Onam, or the everyday morning threshold kolam left for the house and the ants.

Trace on a pulli grid with a kind stroke check. Then draw freely, mirror a line with 4-way or 8-way symmetry, and finish with rice powder, flowers, and diyas.

• 21 languages (same family as Count Mantras): Tamil first among Indian languages, then Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, and more
• Tamil and English names on every pattern
• Guided tracing on a pulli grid — kind, not a quiz
• Pulli kolam, sikku knots, Pongal pot, Onam pookalam, and more (16 original geometries)
• Dot-grid, freehand, and template studios
• Rice powder, flower, and diya decoration
• Save, favorite, duplicate, and share from an on-device gallery

Artwork stays on your device. No account. A banner sits on the main tabs, and a full-screen ad may appear after you finish a rangoli. After a few completed designs, Apple's in-app rating card may appear; you can also rate from Profile.

Works on iPhone (portrait) and iPad (portrait and landscape).
