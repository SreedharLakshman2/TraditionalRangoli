# Privacy Policy — Traditional Rangoli

**Last updated:** August 25, 2026  
**App name:** Traditional Rangoli  
**Developer:** Sai Laksha Technologies  
**Bundle ID:** com.sreedhar.TraditionalRangoli  

## Overview

Traditional Rangoli is a rangoli and kolam learning app. Your drawings, favorites, and progress stay **on your device**. The app does not require an account. The app is free and shows **third-party ads**.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores locally:

- Rangoli drawings, thumbnails, and favorites (files in the app’s Documents folder)
- XP, streak, completed patterns, sound, haptics, grid, and onboarding preferences (UserDefaults)

This is **not** sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

## Advertising

We use **Google AdMob** to show a banner at the bottom of the main tabs and a full-screen interstitial after you complete a rangoli.

On iOS 14.5 and later we may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission so ads can be more relevant. You can decline. The app still works, and ads can still appear.

Google may collect a device identifier and related advertising data according to Google’s policies, to show and measure ads. That data is not linked to an account we hold.

Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy  
Google’s advertising page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads  

Authorized sellers: https://sreedharlakshman2.github.io/app-ads.txt

## Information we do not collect ourselves

We do **not**:

- Require an account or login  
- Collect your name, email, photos library, or precise location for our servers  
- Use our own analytics or crash reporter  
- Sell or upload your artwork to our servers  

If you use Share, iOS’s share sheet may send the image you choose to the app you pick (Messages, Mail, and so on). That is under that app’s privacy policy.

## Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are provided by Google; we do not target children.

## Data retention and deletion

Delete the app to remove local drawings and progress.
