# Privacy Policy — Traditional Rangoli

**Last updated:** August 25, 2026  
**App name:** Traditional Rangoli  
**Developer:** Sai Laksha Technologies  
**Bundle ID:** com.sreedhar.TraditionalRangoli  

## Overview

Traditional Rangoli is a rangoli and kolam learning app. Your drawings, favorites, and progress stay **on your device**. The app does not require an account and does **not** show ads.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores locally:

- Rangoli drawings, thumbnails, and favorites (files in the app’s Documents folder)
- XP, streak, completed patterns, sound, haptics, grid, and onboarding preferences (UserDefaults)

This is **not** sent to Sai Laksha Technologies.

## Information we do not collect

We do **not**:

- Require an account or login  
- Collect your name, email, photos library, or precise location  
- Use analytics, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs  
- Sell or upload your artwork to our servers  

If you use Share, iOS’s share sheet may send the image you choose to the app you pick (Messages, Mail, and so on). That is under that app’s privacy policy.

## Children’s privacy

The app is for a general audience including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention and deletion

Delete the app to remove local drawings and progress.
